One image summing up the world's news of Monday, 23 November 2026,
drawn as a newspaper front page. An AI judge compares the images two
at a time and picks the one that best sums up the day.

## The day in the image

- **COP31 collapses without a deal** — the longest UN climate talks in
  history end in Antalya with no agreement, split over fossil fuels
  and finance.
- **Ukraine sinks a Russian warship off Libya** — Zelensky confirms sea
  drones hit the corvette Mercury at Benghazi.
- **Russian strikes kill 15 near Kyiv** — a boy is pulled alive from
  the rubble; 14 people are missing in Dymer.
- **Factory fire kills at least 24 in Indonesia** — a blaze at a sweets
  factory leaves dozens missing.
- **Nvidia brings back Jensen Huang** — shares jump about 30% as the
  board reinstates its founder as chief executive.
- Also today: the EU proposes a 22nd Russia sanctions package; the NHS
  begins an AI breast-screening trial; BepiColombo enters orbit around
  Mercury after eight years.

## Run

`main.ts` (Node 22) copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
The image is 1024×1024 PNG, drawn fresh for this entry.
